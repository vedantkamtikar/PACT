JUDGES FAQ CHEATSHEET (PREPARED ANSWERS FOR Q&A):
================================================================================
Q1: "Why not just use an LLM to decide when to retry?"
A1: "Because banking regulation requires deterministic guarantees. An LLM can 
hallucinate or drift; an `if (in_blackout) return False` check cannot. We use 
LLMs where language is unstructured (parsing raw bank codes & speaking Hinglish), 
and pure deterministic state machines where money and law are involved."

Q2: "What happens if a user breaks their Promise-to-Pay?"
A2: "If the promised debit fails, PACT evaluates remaining retries against the 
3-retry NPCI cap. If all 3 attempts have been exhausted, the mandate automatically 
transitions to 'STOPPING_RULE_MAX_RETRIES' — halting all debit activity and 
requesting voluntary customer re-authorization."

Q3: "How does this integrate with Razorpay?"
A3: "PACT consumes failed webhook payloads from Razorpay's Subscriptions and 
AutoPay APIs (`subscription.charged`, `payment.failed`), runs the orchestration, 
and triggers subsequent debits via Razorpay's Recurring Invoices endpoint."
